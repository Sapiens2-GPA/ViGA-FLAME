ViGA-FLAME:
Aligning Latent Visual and Geometric Representations for Robust 3D Head Expression Estimation

Wen Hao He, Jiacheng Wu, Jie Chen†
Hong Kong Baptist University

Abstract

Estimating 3D head expression from a single image requires linking visual appearance to facial geometry. We propose ViGA-FLAME, a two-stage framework that aligns semantic visual features with a structured geometric latent space. First, a Parametric–Geometric Variational Autoencoder (PG-VAE) learns a unified parametric-geometric representation of canonical head surfaces and FLAME parameters, supporting both detailed surface reconstruction and interpretable parameter recovery. Supervision on geometry and parameters, together with consistency between the two decoding branches, connects surface detail with parametric structure. Second, a trainable Visual-Geometric Adapter (ViGA) maps features from a pretrained semantic visual encoder into this latent space. Asymmetric attention allows the adapter to read contextual features while preserving the encoder’s original semantic outputs. ViGA-FLAME brings together complementary cues from visual semantics and geometric structure to support coherent 3D head expression estimation under challenging conditions, including extreme expressions and viewing angles, outperforming competing methods.

Pipeline

Method overview. Our framework has two stages. PG-VAE first learns a shared representation of canonical FLAME surfaces and decodes it into both ordered 3D geometry and interpretable FLAME parameters. ViGA then uses asymmetric attention to map features from a frozen Sapiens2 image encoder into the same geometric latent space. Finally, the frozen PG-VAE decoder reconstructs the head surface and FLAME parameters from a single image.
